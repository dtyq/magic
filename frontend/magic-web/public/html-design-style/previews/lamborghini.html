Carbon Monofuselage Breakdown

A revolutionary chassis architecture delivering +25% torsional stiffness over the Aventador with a 10% weight reduction.

Forged Composites Tub
Made from carbon fibers soaked in resin and cured in heated presses, enabling complex structural geometries unattainable via woven carbon.
Central Battery Backbone
The high-voltage battery occupies the central structural tunnel formerly reserved for the transmission shaft, maintaining optimal 44:56 weight distribution.
Transverse 8-Speed DCT
Positioned transversely behind the longitudinal V12 engine to liberate cabin legroom and shorten rear packaging overhangs.

Y-Shape Aero Matrix

The distinctive front DRLs, side air blades, and hexagonal tailpipes establish a coherent aerodynamic language channeling +61% rear downforce.

Active Rear Wing

Three automated aerodynamic positions: Low Drag for straight-line terminal velocity, High Downforce for cornering stability, and Airbrake deceleration.

Torque Vectoring 2.0

Twin electric motors on the front axle individually distribute torque in milliseconds, eliminating understeer and optimizing apex agility.
