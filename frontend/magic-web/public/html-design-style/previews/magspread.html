DISCOURSE // TEXTILE PHILOSOPHY ESSAY BY VALENTINA MORI
CURATOR’S MARGINALIA

The tension between structural rigidity and fluid motion reflects our wider cultural anxiety concerning technological prosthetics.

CHROMATIC PALETTE

Fashion has long ceased to function merely as covering. In the hands of contemporary atelier sculptors, garment construction has transformed into an aggressive architectural argument regarding space, volume, and bodily containment.

Where 20th-century couture sought to flatter or drape, the post-digital atelier builds cantilevered scaffolds. The silhouette no longer concedes to the anatomy; instead, the body is reconfigured as an internal armature supporting an autonomous kinetic sculpture.

“True haute couture does not decorate the body; it constructs a new monument upon it.”
— VALENTINA MORI, CURATOR OF APPLIED ARTS
