# TeamSync

Warm, collaborative, quietly productive.

## Overview

TeamSync is a design system designed for project management and team collaboration platforms where human connection drives productivity. The aesthetic is warm and inviting — violet primary tones inspire creativity, rose accents draw attention to people and activity, and generous spacing gives conversations and tasks room to breathe.

## Colors

- **Primary** (#7C3AED): Primary actions, active navigation, project accents
- **Secondary** (#F43F5E): Notifications, mentions, urgent items, presence dots
- **Tertiary** (#84CC16): Completed tasks, positive feedback, progress indicators
- **Background** (#FAFAF9): App-level canvas with warm undertone
- **Surface** (#FFFFFF): Cards, modals, task panels
- **Success** (#22C55E)
- **Warning** (#F59E0B)
- **Error** (#EF4444)
- **Info** (#7C3AED)

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do show team member presence and avatars near tasks and comments to reinforce collaboration
- Do use warm stone neutrals instead of cold grays — warmth is central to the TeamSync personality
- Don't place more than three action buttons on a single card — prioritize the primary action clearly
