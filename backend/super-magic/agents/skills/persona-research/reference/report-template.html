Conclusions by hypothesis

This study informs one decision: whether to redesign the first-week onboarding.

Hypothesis 1 Partly supported

Trial users do not form a weekly routine

Some completed AI interviews describe trying the app and then forgetting it, which fits a missing routine; why users leave is not yet confirmed. No external source yet covers behaviour in the second week.[Q1][S1]

Judging criteria

Supported if most interviewees describe no recurring use. Refuted if most name a missing feature as the reason they stopped.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The interviewees are AI-simulated personas, not real users. Their answers reflect the persona profiles and the model's general knowledge.
  • Effective sample: 7 of 8 planned interviews were completed; 1 failed because the persona profile was incomplete.
  • Social media evidence was collected through search engines only, so it does not cover the platforms in full.
  • Persona traits marked "(inferred)" in the transcripts are model assumptions, not sourced facts. One interview with a high share of inferred answers was given less weight.
  • An AI sample cannot support statistical inference. "Most", "some" and "a few" describe the AI interviews only.
  • Two quotes that could not be matched word for word to the transcripts were removed during the quote check.
